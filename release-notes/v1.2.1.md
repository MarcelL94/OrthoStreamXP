## Fixes

- **Photo scenery missing on the first flight after installing.** If you ticked **Launch OrthoStreamXP now** at the end of the installer and then pressed **Start XP**, X-Plane started without the photo scenery and removed the OrthoStream packages from its scenery list. Restarting OrthoStreamXP made it work. **Start XP** now starts X-Plane the same way a double-click in Windows does, so the first flight shows the photo scenery too. Your **X-Plane start options** are still passed to X-Plane as before.
