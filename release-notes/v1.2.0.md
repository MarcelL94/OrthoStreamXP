## What's new

- **Ground detail.** X-Plane draws its own procedural grass, shrubs and dirt over the ground up close. On sharp aerial photos this looks like noise: dark speckles and pale stony patches that the real imagery does not have. It is now off by default, so the photos look as they do on the map. If you like some extra texture when flying low, raise **Ground detail** in **Settings › Quality**: anything between 10 and 90 % is a lighter version of the effect, and 100 % is X-Plane's full effect. Your scenery packages are not changed.

## Good to know

- After updating, the ground up close looks cleaner than before. To get the old look back, set **Ground detail** to 100 %.
